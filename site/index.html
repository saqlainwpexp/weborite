How it works

From a Google Maps search to a live WordPress site, in one app.

Each step picks up where the last one stopped, so the lead you found in the morning can be a proposal by the afternoon.

  1. FindSearch Google Maps by trade and city. Each business gets a fit score.
  2. Mock upClaude redesigns their homepage from their own text, photos and colours.
  3. PitchSend the mockup to the owner on WhatsApp or by email.
  4. BuildTurn the approved homepage into a full multi-page site.
  5. WordPressConvert it to editable Elementor pages on the client's WordPress.
  6. Launch & careCheck SEO and speed, go live, then keep it updated and secure.

Features

Everything an agency does for a small business client.

Mockups built from the business's real content

The app captures the current website, including text, photos, logo, colours, reviews and phone numbers. Claude then designs a new homepage around them. A quality check rejects designs with poor contrast, broken layouts or claims that aren't on the original site, so you never send a prospect something embarrassing.

  • Design systems per industry, starting with HVAC, plumbing and restaurants

Lead Finder

Search Google Maps for any trade in any city. Sort by fit score, reviews or rating, select the best ones and create mockups in bulk, or export them to CSV.

Automations

Run a campaign on its own: search a niche, pick the best-fit businesses and have their mockups ready when you sit down.

Full site builds

Extend an approved homepage into service, about and contact pages in the same design.

Launch & SEO

Speed, accessibility and SEO checks before go-live, with fixes you can apply from the app.

Real WordPress, not a page builder export

The site is rebuilt as native Elementor pages on the client's own WordPress, so they can edit every heading, image and button. Works with and without Elementor Pro.

  • WooCommerce stores: Stripe, PayPal, Mollie, bank transfer and cash on delivery
  • Import up to 500 products from a CSV, with categories, variations and images
  • Flat-rate shipping zones and free-shipping thresholds

Maintenance

Watch every client site for updates and known vulnerabilities. One click installs Wordfence and sets up brute-force protection.

Communication

Connect Facebook lead forms and WhatsApp, so new enquiries arrive in the app as leads with a mockup on the way.

Agency dashboard

See your clients, projects and revenue in one overview.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask us.

Is the WordPress output real WordPress?

Yes. The site is built as native Elementor pages on the client's own WordPress install. Your client logs in to wp-admin and edits text, images and buttons like any other site. There is no locked page, iframe or hosted copy.

Do I need Elementor Pro?

No. Conversions work with the free Elementor plugin. If Elementor Pro is installed, the app also uses Pro widgets such as the nav menu and forms.

Does it copy other websites or templates?

No. Each mockup uses the prospect's own content and an industry design system, and the quality check blocks any claim that isn't on the business's current site.

Why do I need my own Claude account?

Claude does the design work. Using your own account keeps your prompts and your prospects' content between you and Anthropic, and you only pay for what you use.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer. Leads, mockups, client sites and passwords stay in the app's folder, and passwords are encrypted. We don't run a server that receives them. The privacy policy lists every service the app talks to.

Can I use it on my laptop and my desktop?

One license is active on one computer at a time. To move it, open Settings and deactivate it, then enter the key on the other computer.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Weborite Studio runs on Windows 10 and 11.
