FAQ

Questions agencies ask

Something else? Email hello@weborite.com

Is the WordPress output real WordPress?

Yes. The site is built as native Elementor pages on the client's own WordPress install. Your client logs in to wp-admin and edits text, images and buttons like any other site. There is no locked page, iframe or hosted copy.

Do I need Elementor Pro?

No. Conversions work with the free Elementor plugin. If Elementor Pro is installed, the app also uses Pro widgets such as the nav menu and forms.

Does it copy other websites or templates?

No. Each mockup uses the prospect's own content and an industry design system, and the quality check blocks any claim that isn't on the business's current site.

Why do I need my own Claude account?

Claude does the design work. Using your own account keeps your prompts and your prospects' content between you and Anthropic, and you only pay for what you use.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer. Leads, mockups, client sites and passwords stay in the app's folder, and passwords are encrypted. The privacy policy lists every service the app talks to.

Can I use it on my laptop and my desktop?

One license is active on one computer at a time. To move it, open Settings and deactivate it, then enter the key on the other computer.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Weborite Studio runs on Windows 10 and 11.
