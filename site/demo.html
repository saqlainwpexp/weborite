Get started

Up and running in five minutes

  1. Download

    Get the installer above. It's a single file for Windows 10 and 11.

  2. Install

    Open it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Choose the demo

    On the first screen, click Try the demo first, then add your name and agency. They appear on your mockups.

  4. Connect Claude

    In Settings, sign in with your Claude account or paste an Anthropic API key. Then run your first search.

Demo questions

Before you download

Something else? Email hello@weborite.com

Does the demo expire?

No. There's no time limit, only the allowances above. They're counted for good, so deleting something doesn't give it back.

What happens when I use an allowance up?

That one feature stops and the app offers to unlock it. Everything else keeps working, and nothing you made is lost.

Do I keep my work when I buy?

Yes. Click Enter license key at the top of the app and paste your key. Every limit is removed without reinstalling.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The demo needs only your name and email, which stay on your computer.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Windows shows a warning for new apps from small publishers. Click More info, then Run anyway. The app only talks to the services listed in the privacy policy.
