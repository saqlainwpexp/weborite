Questions before you buy

Something else? Email hello@weborite.com

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The free demo includes every feature with small limits. When you buy, enter your key in the demo and your work stays.

Why an invoice instead of a card checkout?

We sell directly, so you can pay by bank transfer, Payoneer or Wise, including from countries card checkouts often don't support.

How does renewing work?

We send a renewal invoice before your license ends. If you don't renew, the app asks for an active key when the period is over, and your data stays on your computer.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment. See the refund policy.

Can I use one license on two computers?

One license is active on one computer at a time. Deactivate it in Settings to move it to another computer.

Do I need to pay for Claude separately?

Yes. The designs are made with your own Claude subscription or Anthropic API key, billed by Anthropic.
